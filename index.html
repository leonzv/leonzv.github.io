$./analyze--approach

I enjoy turning complex operations into simple, reliable systems. The code comes last: first I find who is affected, what the real constraint is and which rule the business actually needs.

  1. 01Understand the operation
  2. 02Find the real constraint
  3. 03Agree the rule with the business
  4. 04Simplify, then verify
Problem solving
Separate the symptom from the cause, find the real constraint and pick the simplest fix that holds up in daily operation.
Strategic thinking
Weigh the quick fix against what the operation will need next, and make the trade-offs visible before choosing.
Process optimization
Map how work really happens, remove steps and rework, and keep what people actually use.
Product discovery
Understand who uses the system and what they are trying to do before deciding what to build.
Requirements analysis
Turn vague requests and edge cases into explicit rules the business has agreed to and tests can check.
Technology consulting
Explain options and trade-offs in plain language, so decisions are made by the people who own them.
Digital innovation
Replace manual or fragile steps with something simpler that people trust.
Systems integration
Connect devices, services and data so they behave as one system, including when something fails or restarts.
